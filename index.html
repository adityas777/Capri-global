How these numbers are defined

Connected & audited calls had enough conversation for the AI audit; all rates are a share of these. Quality score is the audit’s normalised score out of 10, and a call with any fatal error scores 0. PTP counts outcomes Promise To Pay, Penal PTP and NACH PTP; the median PTP amount uses PTP calls with an amount captured. A breakdown chart ignores its own filter, so you can always see and switch between its options. Data: November 2026 calls (2–18 Nov) from the call-report export of 19 Nov 2026.
