Agent-level improvements

  1. Fix disposition tagging first. Run a short refresher on matching the tag to what actually happened on the call, and spot-check tags daily for the agents at the top of “Agents to coach”.
  2. Coach on the weakest check per agent, using the tip in the table, and review their fatal calls together from “Calls to review”.
  3. Catch the weekly slide early. Watch the agent trend table every week; any agent whose score falls by more than one point gets a call review within the week.
  4. Pair weaker agents with the top scorers for call listening sessions on closing and empathy.

Lead-level improvements

  1. Follow up more. Leads called several times promise and pay far more often, yet half of leads get one call. Schedule a second call for every lead without a promise or payment.
  2. Chase promises before the due date. Call leads that promised to pay a day before the promised date, and again on the day if no payment is seen.
  3. Keep one owner per lead where possible. Use “Lead results by agent” to compare agents on the same lead types before reassigning.
  4. Route bank or mandate issues to operations rather than repeated collection calls, and track them as a separate queue.

Call-level improvements

  1. Use a fixed opening: own name, Capri Global, customer name, and the amount and due date within the first 15 seconds.
  2. Don’t end calls under a minute without an ask. Always ask for a specific amount and date and offer a payment link before closing.
  3. Close every call the same way: repeat the promise (amount and date), confirm the payment mode, and thank the customer.
  4. On long calls, stay on script. Longer calls carry more fatal errors, so keep a checklist: no threats, verify identity before details, and tag the right outcome at the end.

How these numbers are defined

Connected & audited calls had enough conversation for the AI audit; all rates are a share of these. Quality score is the audit’s normalised score out of 10, and a call with any fatal error scores 0. PTP counts outcomes Promise To Pay, Penal PTP and NACH PTP; the median PTP amount uses PTP calls with an amount captured. Awareness campaigns are campaigns whose name starts with “PDM”; every other campaign is a collection campaign. Attended means the dialer marked the call Connected (the customer picked up); call length bands use the full call duration. Loan and mobile numbers are masked to their last 4 digits, and no emails are included. A breakdown chart ignores its own filter, so you can always see and switch between its options.
